    • The stand banner and the action card are fixed height (148 px and 262 px min), so nothing below moves when the words change.
    • The stand word changes only when the strength crosses ±40 to enter and ±20 to leave, and holds 90 seconds. On 13 Nov the old read flipped HOLD and WATCH eight times in 25 minutes on ₹1 to ₹3 of premium.
    • The action card is fed by the engine at 1 Hz (under 1 s a step), not by the forecaster, which ran up to 12 s behind and was stale for about 52 minutes on 13 Nov. When the forecaster is stale the stand banner greys out with its age; the action card keeps working.
    • The ± range moves out of the hero: the ladder keeps it for anyone who wants it, but the headline is a word, a number and a sentence.
    • Every percentage on this panel is a measured record with its sample, never a model's raw output.
    • Colours keep their meaning: green buyers / calls, coral sellers / puts, gold only when a setup is armed or a buy fires, amber near an exit.
