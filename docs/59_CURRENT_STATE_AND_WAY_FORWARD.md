## 1. Summary

* **What trades the account:** one Conductor (`apex/engine/conductor.py`) mirroring the first eligible trigger of five
  books that are all variants of one pattern, the c20 pullback (normal days) and breakout (expiry days). Paper only,
  ₹1,00,000 fresh every day, all-in on every trade. Unchanged since 9 Nov.
* **Live record since 9 Nov** (E1): +59.7 %, +7.4 %, +3.7 %, -27.1 % on four sessions; 22 trades, 9 winners.
* **What a year of evidence says** (E4, E7, E8): the champion's normal-day edge is about +0.65 % a trade with 38 %
  winners (year before the recorded window, per second, real strategy classes) against +7.75 % in the recorded four
  weeks; on expiry days it is -0.62 % a trade with 11 of 48 days up. With the account reset to ₹1,00,000 every day,
  as it runs live (E9), the year's normal days add up to +249 % of a day's capital, the 48 expiry days to -579 %, so
  the champion as it trades today came to -331 % (-1.45 % on the average day). (Corrected 14 Nov: an earlier version
  of this report carried capital across the whole year, which is not the live account, and showed it going to zero.)
* **What helps:** buying against a stretched move (three "fade" books). Pooled with the champion in one account, with
  no pause between trades as the live Conductor works, it made +550 % against +249 % on the same normal days (E9),
  and the fades are the only idea family positive in both halves of four years (E5, E6). They run as shadows from
  Monday.
* **The most urgent fix is not a strategy:** the live engine ran far slower than its budget on 13 Nov (median step
  72 ms, 5,038 steps over 300 ms, 20 % of steps dropped; E2) while research jobs shared the machine.
* **Owner's decisions on record:** the champion stays live on expiry days (14 Nov, "Keep it live"); all-in, never
  position sizing (standing rule); new logic as shadows judged by the 7-test standard (docs/28).

(`apex/engine/learning.py:595`, and the challengers' paired test at line 627 uses the same start). With the owner's

### 4.3 The account as it runs live (E9, `s48_parity.py`)

Corrected 14 Nov after a second, independent review (`docs/57_SYSTEM_PROFITABILITY_REVIEW.md`, written by another
tool in this repository) found two faults in the first version: it compounded capital across the whole year, while
the live account starts at ₹1,00,000 every day (`config.START_CAPITAL`), and its merge of several books into one
account added a 60-second pause after every exit that the live Conductor does not have. Both checked and fixed:

| Year before the recorded window | Trades | Per trade | Sum of daily results, % of a day's capital | Mean day | Days up | Worst day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Champion, normal days | 393 | +0.65 % | +249 | +1.38 % | 56 of 135 | -45 % |
| Champion, 48 expiry days (real option seconds) | 639 | -0.62 % | -579 | -12.07 % | 11 of 48 | -84 % |
| **Champion on every day, as it trades live** | **1,032** | **-0.13 %** | **-331** | **-1.45 %** | **67 of 183** | **-84 %** |
| Champion + three fades, normal days | 662 | +1.07 % | +550 | +3.06 % | 78 of 180 | -47 % |
| Champion + fade60 + orbfade, normal days | 621 | +1.21 % | +589 | +3.27 % | 75 of 180 | -43 % |
| fade60 + orbfade without the champion, normal days | 299 | +2.04 % | +620 | +3.44 % | 82 of 180 | -39 % |

Within each day the account is all-in, so a day's trades compound; across days nothing carries. On that convention
the normal days are positive and the expiry days decide the year. (Carried across the whole year instead, the same
trades shrink: an edge under about +2 % a trade loses to the swing of each trade; that is the variance cost of
compounding and the reason the first version looked worse. It is shown in the last column of E9.)
